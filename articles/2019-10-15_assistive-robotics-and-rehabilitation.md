# Assistive Robotics and Rehabilitation

- **Published:** 2019-10-15 on gtec.at
- **Original:** https://www.gtec.at/2019/10/15/assistive-robotics-and-rehabilitation/

> **Summary:** José M. Azorín is the Director of the Brain-Machine Interface Systems Lab and Associate Professor of the Systems Engineering and Automation Department at Miguel Hernández University of Elche in Spain.

---

José M. Azorín is the Director of the Brain-Machine Interface Systems Lab and Associate Professor of the Systems Engineering and Automation Department at Miguel Hernández University of Elche in Spain.

He studied Computer Science at the University of Alicante and did his PhD on Tele-robotics at the Miguel Hernández University of Elche. His current research interests are Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs), Neurorobotics, Assistive Robotics and Rehabilitation Robotics. We had the chance to talk with him about his work and research.
