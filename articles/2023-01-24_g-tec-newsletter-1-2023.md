## REMOVE ARTEFACTS WITH O-S-C-A-R

Stop wasting time, enable BCI experiments under difficult conditions

## Default

OSCAR is a real-time pre-processing module that removes motion, cable, blink, and other artifacts from EEG signals. It saves valuable data and time and enables BCI experiments under difficult conditions. OSCAR is suitable for anyone who wants to focus on the EEG instead of wasting much time cleaning it. OSCAR is soon available in the g.HIsys software environment.

SINGLE FINGER MOVEMENT DECODING WITH NON-INVASIVE EEG

## A publication in Frontiers for Neuroscience

What an accomplishment! In this image, you see how single fingers are decoded over the motor cortex with the g.PANGOLIN Ultra High-Density EEG system.

The brown bubbles indicate the thumb; the red bubbles indicate the index finger; the yellow bubbles indicate the middle finger; the green bubbles indicate the ring finger; the blue bubbles indicate the little finger. The small rings represent 1024 single g.PANGOLIN electrodes. The dark grey rings represent a clinical recording system with 21 EEG electrodes only (10/20 system). The results speak for themselves: g.PANGOLIN gives you a much higher spacial resolution.
