## g.AUDIOBOX – TRIGGER & STIMULATION

## Compatible with g.tec’s biosignal amplifiers and headsets

The g.AUDIOBOX is an auditory stimulation device that allows researchers to perform perfect stereo-stimulation for precise auditory evoked potentials. No jitter, no time-delay, just precise timing between the stimulation and the EEG data.

Together with the g.tec Suite 2020, it is a ready-to-use stimulation unit to test auditory awareness and cognitive functions in less time. g.AUDIOBOX is available in the g.tec product configurator.

## IMPEDANCE CHECK IN 2D & 3D

Watch the video about the new software feature in the g.HIsys and g.Recorder software Default Acquire high-quality EEG easily with the new Impedance Check! The electrode impedance describes how well the contact between the electrode and the skin of the human scalp is established with gel. The 2D and 3D views make it much easier to identify electrode locations with high impedances. Then, simply add more gel until the EEG electrode shows a green color.

Get the g.HIsys or g.Recorder software modules to use the new Impedance Check for your measurements from the g.tec shop!

## CCEP MAPPING WITH CORTIQ SYSTEM

Identify the language network with CCEP mappings

## Default

This experiment was conducted with Dr. Kyousuke Kamada in the ICU and an epilepsy patient. His intention was to identify the language network by stimulating the Broca’s area, Wernicke’s area and the auditory cortex. He was also able to identify pathological CCEPs and interrupt seizure pathways effectively in his neurosurgery interventions. g.tec’s user-ready cortiQ brain mapping system allows neurosurgeons to map cortico-cortical evoked potentials of the whole cortex using the g.HIamp biosignal amplifier, g.Estim PRO cortical stimulator together with the Switching Unit to induce CCEPs.
