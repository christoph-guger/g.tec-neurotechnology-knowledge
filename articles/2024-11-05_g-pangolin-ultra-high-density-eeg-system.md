## A Leap Forward for Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) Technology

BCI technology enables users to control devices using brain signals, without any physical movement. Traditional EEG systems often struggle with limited spatial resolution, which can hinder the accuracy required for decoding fine motor movements such as finger gestures. g.Pangolin’s ultra-high-density EEG (uHD EEG) system overcomes this limitation, offering features specifically designed for BCI applications:

Ultra-High Density Electrode Array: With 256 channels spaced at just 8.6 mm, g.Pangolin provides more data points than traditional EEG systems (which typically have a 60-65 mm inter-electrode distance), allowing for precise decoding of fine motor movements.

Flexible Electrode Grids: The grids conform closely to the scalp, improving spatial resolution and body motion decoding.

High Classification Accuracy: g.Pangolin achieves an average classification accuracy of 64.8%, with up to 70.6% accuracy for specific finger movements, enabling a more refined and responsive BCI experience.

This technology represents a major step forward in BCI, enabling accurate control of external devices through thought alone and supporting groundbreaking research in neuroscience, neurorehabilitation, and beyond.
