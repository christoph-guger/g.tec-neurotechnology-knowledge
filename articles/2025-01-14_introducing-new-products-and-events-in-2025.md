## The New g.Nautilus NIRx System

A Powerful, Wireless, High-Performance Solution for Advanced Neuroscience and Brain Imaging g.Nautilus NIRX system revolutionizes brain data acquisition by enabling the simultaneous, high-quality recording of EEG and fNIRS signals in real-time. This portable, wearable solution integrates seamlessly with g.HIsys Professional for synchronized data streaming, real-time analysis, and advanced post-experiment insights through g.BSanalyze.

Designed for flexibility, the system offers configurations with up to 64 EEG channels and 32 fNIRS optode holders, all housed in the comfortable g.GAMMAcap. Its innovative design makes it a game-changer for neuroscience research in both naturalistic and clinical settings.

**Key Highlights:** Seamless Real-Time Integration:

Stream, process, and analyze synchronized EEG and fNIRS data in real time with g.HIsys Professional.

**Customizable and Comfortable:** Configurable with up to 64 EEG channels and 32 optodes, all integrated into the ergonomic g.GAMMAcap.

**Versatile Applications:** Ideal for BCIs, cognitive workload monitoring, neurorehabilitation, and real-world neuroscience experiments.

## The New Super-Flat EEG Electrodes for TMS Recordings

Advanced TMS-EEG Integration g.LADYbird TMS-EEG electrodes, available in active and passive versions, are specifically designed for combined EEG and TMS setups. These ultra-flat, 3 mm-thick electrodes allow the TMS coil to be positioned closer to the brain, reducing artifacts and ensuring superior signal quality. With seamless integration into g.GAMMAcap or adhesive washers, the electrodes deliver exceptional performance for cutting-edge neuroscience research.

**Key Highlights:** Ultra-Thin Design:

At just 3 mm, the thinnest on the market, they ensure optimal coil placement for minimal TMS artifacts.

**Active and Passive Options:** Choose active electrodes for high-impedance recordings or passive electrodes for minimized stimulation artifacts.

**Optimal Signal Resolution:** Made with sintered Ag/AgCl material, they deliver precise EEG data up to 10 kHz.

## The New g.HIamp NIRx System

Combine NIRSport2 with g.HIamp for Advanced EEG Sampling and Expanded Channel Capability The g.HIamp NIRX system sets a new standard in neuroimaging by enabling the simultaneous recording of high-quality EEG and fNIRS signals, ensuring synchronized and comprehensive brain data collection.

With 256 EEG channels and 32 detectors/sources for fNIRS acquisition, this powerful system integrates seamlessly with g.HIsys Professional for real-time data streaming, processing, and analysis. Post-experiment insights are enhanced with g.BSanalyze, providing researchers with an all-in-one solution for cutting-edge neuroscience studies in real-world or clinical settings.

**Key Highlights:** Unmatched Channel Capacity:

Features 256 EEG channels and 32 fNIRS optodes for comprehensive spatial resolution and synchronized data acquisition.

**Seamless Data Integration:** Real-time streaming, processing, and classification via g.HIsys Professional ensure precise and efficient data handling.

**Versatile Applications:** Ideal for BCIs, neurorehabilitation, and cognitive workload studies, offering rich, synchronized insights into brain activity.

## The New Unicorn BCI Core System

Versatile EEG Recordings for Humans and Animals

## The

Unicorn BCI Core-8 is a groundbreaking 24-bit, 8-channel EEG and BCI device designed for versatile neural interface applications in humans, dogs, horses, and more. Its magnetic attachment to customizable base stations ensures adaptability across diverse recording scenarios.

With options for gold cup or g.SAHARA Hybrid electrodes, the device delivers reliable signal quality, even in challenging conditions like thick hair or fur. Streaming data into g.HIsys enables real-time visualization and signal processing, while the g.Pype SDK provides powerful Python-based tools for advanced EEG analysis.

**Key Highlights:** Versatile and Adaptable:

Designed for humans and animals with configurable base stations and electrode options.

**Powerful Signal Processing:** Supports real-time EEG visualization and Python-based analysis with g.Pype SDK.

**Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Fully compatible with macOS and iOS, ensuring seamless integration across devices.

**High-Quality Data:** Offers 250 Hz and 1000 Hz sampling rates for EEG, ECG, and EMG applications.
