## Educational Journey and Founding g.tec

Could you briefly tell us about your background and how it led you to start your business?

I studied Biomedical Engineering and had the incredible opportunity to further my education at Johns Hopkins University. During my time there, I discovered the work of Dr. Gert Pfurtscheller, a renowned professor from my home university—the University of Technology Graz in Austria—who was pioneering research in brain-computer interfaces (BCIs).

At that time, BCI research was still in its infancy, with only three active labs worldwide. Intrigued by this cutting-edge field, I returned to Graz, where I completed my master’s thesis under Dr. Pfurtscheller’s mentorship and later pursued my PhD in the same domain.

This journey fueled my passion for advancing BCI technology, ultimately inspiring me to establish g.tec. My goal has always been to push the boundaries of neurotechnology, making it more accessible and impactful for researchers, developers, and clinicians worldwide. g.tec: Leading the Charge in BCI Technology What does your business focus on, and how is it positioned in the market?

Our business specializes in developing state-of-the-art brain-computer interface (BCI) and neurotechnology solutions that empower researchers, clinicians, and innovators to unlock the brain’s potential. Our products cater to a wide range of applications, including neuroscience research, clinical rehabilitation, education, and interactive technologies. g.tec is a global leader in the BCI field, with over two decades of expertise. We distinguish ourselves by offering a comprehensive product portfolio that includes high-quality EEG systems, real-time signal processing software, and groundbreaking applications such as recoveriX for stroke rehabilitation and mindBEAGLE for assessing consciousness.

Our solutions stand out for their ease of use, superior signal quality, and robust customization options through APIs, attracting a diverse audience ranging from top researchers and universities to companies and developers creating next-generation technologies.

Through continuous innovation and strong collaborations with academic and industry leaders, g.tec remains at the forefront of the rapidly evolving field of neurotechnology and BCI.

## Expanding Horizons: Plans for Growth

## What are your plans for growth or expansion in the near future?

Our growth plans revolve around advancing our technologies and entering new clinical and research domains. With recoveriX, our stroke rehabilitation system, we’re achieving remarkable results and expanding its applications. New clinical studies reveal its effectiveness not only for stroke patients but also for individuals with multiple sclerosis (MS) and Parkinson’s disease. Additionally, case studies have shown significant improvements in patients with spinal cord injuries, highlighting recoveriX’s versatility.

In the area of brain mapping, we’re introducing a revolutionary system capable of measurements with 1,024 EEG electrodes, offering unprecedented spatial resolution. This breakthrough will enable researchers to revisit and refine previous studies, unveiling new insights into brain activity and function.

With cortiQ, we are integrating electrical stimulation to enhance brain mapping for neurosurgeons. This innovation helps identify eloquent cortical areas with speed and precision, even in pediatric patients, significantly improving surgical outcomes.

By continually expanding our technology’s capabilities and applications, we aim to push the boundaries of neurotechnology and improve lives across clinical, research, and medical fields.

## Milestones and Future Goals

## How has this year impacted your business, and what are your goals moving forward?

This year has been transformative for g.tec, marked by significant milestones in product development and global expansion. One of our proudest achievements has been establishing new recoveriX centers in key markets such as Switzerland, India, Hong Kong, Australia, and beyond. These centers are making our innovative neurorehabilitation technology accessible to patients worldwide, offering effective treatments for stroke, MS, Parkinson’s, and spinal cord injuries.

We also introduced the Unicorn BCI Core-8, a versatile device enabling users to quickly program their own applications in Python. This system is not only ideal for human EEG measurements but also supports research and applications with dogs and horses, expanding the potential of BCI technology across species.

Another highlight was the continued success of the BCI Spring School, which has become a global hub for education, innovation, and collaboration in the brain-computer interface field. With thousands of attendees, it reflects the growing interest and impact of neurotechnology.

Moving forward, our goals include further global expansion, driving innovation with cutting-edge solutions such as our 1,024-electrode EEG system, and continuing to empower researchers, clinicians, and developers to push the boundaries of neuroscience and neurorehabilitation.

## Advice for Entrepreneurs in the Neurotechnology Space

## What advice would you give to startups or entrepreneurs in your industry?

My advice to startups and entrepreneurs in the neurotechnology and BCI industry is to identify a specific problem you’re passionate about solving and dedicate your energy and resources to it. In such a rapidly evolving and highly technical field, it’s easy to become distracted by attempting to tackle too many areas at once. Instead, define your niche, understand the needs of your target audience, and work tirelessly to create the best possible solution.

Once you’ve established your direction, pursue it with determination. Be prepared to invest time, effort, and dedication to turn your vision into reality. Building strong collaborations with researchers, clinicians, or developers in the field can accelerate your progress and help refine your product or service.

Finally, remain adaptable and innovative. Neurotechnology is a field where advancements happen rapidly, and staying open to change and improvement is essential for long-term success.
