# Ultra-high-density EEG and neural decoding

Non-invasive EEG with 256 to 1,024 channels and 8.6 mm spacing for finger, gesture and visual decoding.

**g.tec systems used:** g.Pangolin, g.HIamp

## Peer-reviewed papers with Christoph Guger as co-author
Result sentences are quoted from each abstract.

### Papers: g.Pangolin / high-density EEG (6 papers)
- **2026, Increasing EEG electrode density improves decoding of visual categories and source localization: an exploratory ultra-high-density EEG study.** *Communications engineering*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44172-026-00611-w)
  - Our results show that higher electrode density facilitates more accurate single-trial classification of visual stimuli, achieving an average accuracy of 73% using uHD EEG and regularized linear discriminant analysis.
  - Additionally, higher electrode density resulted in more detailed topographic activation maps and improved source localization of the face-selective N170 component in the fusiform gyrus.
- **2025, Decoding of image properties from single-trial visual evoked potentials recorded by ultra-high-density EEG.** *Scientific reports*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-18275-5)
  - Study participants were shown a diverse set of random images while 512 channels from the uHD system recorded their EEG over the occipital lobe.
  - Grand average decoding performance across all image properties and subjects yielded a Pearson's r of 0.50 between predicted and actual image property score.
  - Our analyses highlight the importance of electrode density with improvements in decoding performance extending even below 10 mm of inter-electrode distance.
- **2025, Brain-computer-interface-driven artistic expression: real-time cognitive visualization in the pangolin scales animatronic dress and screen dress.** *Frontiers in human neuroscience*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2025.1516776)
- **2024, Neural source reconstruction using a novel ultra-high-density EEG system and vibrotactile stimulation of individual fingers.** *Annual International Conference of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society. IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society. Annual International Conference*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC53108.2024.10782167)
- **2024, Mapping of the central sulcus using non-invasive ultra-high-density brain recordings.** *Scientific reports*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-57167-y)
  - Individual EEG channels could be correctly classified as anterior or posterior to the central sulcus with 95.2% accuracy, which is comparable to accuracies from invasive intracranial recordings.
- **2022, Individual finger movement decoding using a novel ultra-high-density electroencephalography-based brain-computer interface system.** *Frontiers in neuroscience*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.1009878)
  - Low spatial sensor resolution, as found in common EEG systems, can be improved by omitting the conventional standard of EEG electrode distribution (the international 10-20 system) and ordinary mounting structures (e.g., flexible caps).
  - The average classification accuracy over subjects was 64.8 (6.3)%, with the middle versus ring finger resulting in the highest average accuracy of 70.6 (9.4)%.

- **2023, Hand gesture decoding using ultra-high-density EEG** *2023 11th International IEEE/EMBS Conference on Neural Engineering (NER)* (proceedings-article). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ner52421.2023.10123901)
  - The classification of the gestures “rock” vs. “scissors” resulted in the highest classification accuracy for both subjects, with 78.9% for subject S1 and 73.9% for subject S2 on average.
