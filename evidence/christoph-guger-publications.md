# Publications by Christoph Guger (PubMed)

137 peer-reviewed papers co-authored by Christoph Guger, CEO of g.tec medical engineering, as indexed in PubMed on 25 October 2026. Topics: brain-computer interfaces, stroke rehabilitation (recoveriX), disorders of consciousness (mindBEAGLE), functional brain mapping (cortiQ), high-gamma ECoG, and wearable EEG (Unicorn, g.Nautilus).

Many more papers, book chapters and conference papers are not in PubMed. Full and current lists: [PubMed search](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Guger+C%5BAuthor%5D), [ORCID 0000-0001-6468-8500](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6468-8500). Papers by other groups that use the Unicorn are in [unicorn-publications.md](unicorn-publications.md).
