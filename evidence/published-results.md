# Published results with g.tec systems

Each line was checked against the paper's abstract or open-access full text. Device names come from the papers' methods sections where available.

## BCI performance
- **P300 vs motor imagery (g.USBamp):** 89% of 81 people spelled at 80–100% accuracy after about 5 minutes of training, versus 19% with motor imagery ([Neurosci Lett 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neulet.2009.06.045)).
- **Dry vs gel electrodes (g.SAHARA vs gel, g.USBamp):** P300 spelling averaged 90.4% dry vs 91.0% gel; 69.6% vs 72.8% of people reached 100% accuracy ([Front Neurosci 2012](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2012.00060)).
- **SSVEP:** all 53 people could use it, average accuracy 95.5% ([Front Neurosci 2012](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2012.00169)).
- **Home BCI, dry vs gel:** every participant spelled a five-letter word at 100% with both ([Front Neurosci 2017](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2017.00286)).
- **ECoG c-VEP BCI (g.HIamp):** 99.21% online accuracy ([Front Syst Neurosci 2014](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2014.00139)).
- **EEG + fNIRS:** about 8–10% higher motor imagery accuracy than EEG alone ([IEEE SMC 2019](https://doi.org/10.1109/smc.2019.8914083)).

## Ultra-high-density EEG (g.Pangolin)
- Central sulcus localized with 95.2% accuracy ([Sci Rep 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-57167-y)).
- Finger decoding 64.8% average, up to 70.6% ([Front Neurosci 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.1009878)).
- Visual category decoding 73% ([Commun Eng 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44172-026-00611-w)).
- 10-10 layouts about 10% worse than ultra-high-density for hand gestures ([IEEE NER 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/ner52421.2023.10123901)).

## Brain mapping (cortiQ, g.HIamp, g.Estim PRO)
- No false positives, 1.24% false negatives ([EMBC 2013](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC.2013.6611010)).
- 100% sensitivity and specificity vs stimulation in the frontal lobe ([World Neurosurg 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wneu.2014.08.009)).
- Language mapping 90.1% sensitivity, 90.0% specificity ([World Neurosurg 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wneu.2016.09.071)).
- 40% fewer stimulations with high-gamma guidance ([EMBC 2017](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC.2017.8037773)).
- Face areas: 85.7% found with high-gamma vs 53.6% with stimulation ([Front Hum Neurosci 2021](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2021.616591)).
- Epilepsy network surgery: Engel class I in 13 of 14 patients ([Front Neurol 2020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2020.00073)).

## Neurorehabilitation (recoveriX)
- Stroke walking speed +0.19 m/s ([Front Neurosci 2023](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2023.1256077)).
- Upper limb FMA-UE +4.68, lasting 6 months ([Front Neurosci 2020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2020.591435)).
- Upper then lower limb: FMA-UE +4.2, +0.15 m/s, Barthel +5 ([Front Neurosci 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2024.1346607)).
- Multiple sclerosis: TUG −15.5%, 25-foot walk −16.4%, 6-minute walk +37.3 m ([Front Med 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2025.1661972)).

## Disorders of consciousness (mindBEAGLE)
- Locked-in: 9 of 12 communicated; 2 of 3 completely locked-in ([Front Neurosci 2017](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2017.00251)).
- ALS: median 98% accuracy ([Front Neurosci 2018](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2018.00514)).
- Command following in 4 patients diagnosed as unresponsive ([Front Neurosci 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.959339)).
- Recovery prediction R = 0.89 ([Clin Neurophysiol 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinph.2018.02.131)).

## Art and education (Unicorn)
- BCI dress on stage: 97.1% classification ([Front Hum Neurosci 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2025.1516776)).
