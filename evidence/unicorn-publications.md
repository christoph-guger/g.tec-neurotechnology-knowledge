# Peer-reviewed studies that used the Unicorn

Studies by g.tec and by independent groups that recorded EEG with the Unicorn Hybrid Black or Unicorn BCI Core. Each entry links to the paper. Sources: g.tec's [recommended citation list](https://www.gtec.at/recommended-citation-list/) and PubMed, checked 25 October 2026. The list is incomplete; many papers that use the Unicorn do not name it in the title or abstract.

## Validation

- **Pontifex MB, Coffman CA (2023).** *Validation of the g.tec Unicorn Hybrid Black wireless EEG system.* Psychophysiology. Independent study (Michigan State University) against a wired low-impedance lab system: valid spectral measures even without electrode preparation; ERPs need conductive solution. [doi:10.1111/psyp.14320](https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.14320)

## BCI control and robotics

- **An Y, Wong J, Ling SH (2024).** *Development of real-time brain-computer interface control system for robot.* Applied Soft Computing. Unicorn Hybrid Black, 5 subjects, 7 commands: 87.2% offline and 93.12% online accuracy, 67.07 bits/min. [doi:10.1016/j.asoc.2024.111648](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.asoc.2024.111648)
- **Belkacem AN, Lakas A (2021).** *A cooperative EEG-based BCI control system for robot–drone interaction.* IWCMC 2021. P300 BCI with which two users control a robot and a drone. [doi:10.1109/IWCMC51323.2021.9498781](https://doi.org/10.1109/IWCMC51323.2021.9498781)

## P300, engagement and cognitive state

- **Sutaj N, Walchshofer M, Schreiner L, Turchet L, Pretl H, Guger C (2021).** *Evaluating a novel P300-based real-time image ranking BCI.* Frontiers in Computer Science. 21 subjects, up to 100% ranking accuracy in specific tasks. [doi:10.3389/fcomp.2021.661224](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcomp.2021.661224)
- **Natalizio A, Sieghartsleitner S, Schreiner L, et al. (2024).** *Real-time estimation of EEG-based engagement in different tasks.* Journal of Neural Engineering. 23 subjects, 8-channel EEG, about 90% accuracy separating engagement from rest. [doi:10.1088/1741-2552/ad200d](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ad200d)

## Aging and dementia

- **Rutkowski TM, Abe MS, Komendziński T, et al. (2023).** *Machine learning approach for early onset dementia neurobiomarker using EEG network topology features.* Frontiers in Human Neuroscience. [doi:10.3389/fnhum.2023.1155194](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2023.1155194)
- **Rutkowski TM, Komendziński T, Otake-Matsuura M (2023).** *Mild cognitive impairment prediction and cognitive score regression in the elderly using EEG topological data analysis and machine learning with awareness assessed in affective reminiscent paradigm.* Frontiers in Aging Neuroscience. [doi:10.3389/fnagi.2023.1294139](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2023.1294139)

## Art, culture and open data

- **Schreiner L, Wipprecht A, Olyanasab A, Sieghartsleitner S, Pretl H, Guger C (2025).** *Brain–computer-interface-driven artistic expression: real-time cognitive visualization in the pangolin scales animatronic dress and screen dress.* Frontiers in Human Neuroscience. [doi:10.3389/fnhum.2025.1516776](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2025.1516776)
- **Luz María AV et al. (2025).** *EEG data of museum visitors experiencing visual and audiovisual simulations of Edzná, an archaeological site in Mexico.* Data in Brief. Recorded with the Unicorn Hybrid Black. [doi:10.1016/j.dib.2025.111855](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2025.111855)
- **g.tec (2025).** [Unicorn BCI Core-8 EEG datasets](https://github.com/unicorn-bi/Unicorn-BCI-Core-8-EEG-Datasets): open raw and preprocessed recordings (resting state, auditory, visual, motor paradigms).
