# Choosing a g.tec EEG system

Questions people ask, and the system that answers them.

**"I need the best signal quality and many channels in the lab, maybe ECoG or sEEG too."**
g.HIamp: 16–256 channels per device, up to 1,024, 24-bit, up to 38.4 kHz, FDA/CE. Add g.SCARABEO or g.LADYbird active electrodes and a g.GAMMAcap. See [g-hiamp.md](../products/g-hiamp.md).

**"I want EEG with the spatial resolution of an intracranial grid, without surgery."**
g.Pangolin grids (8.6 mm spacing) with g.HIamp. See [g-pangolin.md](../products/g-pangolin.md).

**"My participants move: VR, sports, flight simulators, children, real-world studies."**
g.Nautilus Research (8–64 channels, wireless, under 140 g). With fNIRS or Tobii eye tracking if needed. See [g-nautilus.md](../products/g-nautilus.md).

**"I need wireless EEG in a hospital with fast patient preparation."**
g.Nautilus PRO Flexible (CE, FDA-cleared, swappable dry and wet strands).

**"I record EEG during TMS."**
g.HIamp with 3 mm g.LADYbird TMS electrodes (active or passive).

**"I want to build a BCI app in Python, teach a course or run a hackathon on a small budget."**
Unicorn BCI Core-8 or Hybrid Black with g.Pype. Not for medical use. See [unicorn.md](../products/unicorn.md).

**"I record EEG in dogs, horses or other animals."**
Unicorn BCI Core-8 with animal headstraps and gold-cup electrodes.

**"Our neurosurgery team needs functional mapping before resection."**
cortiQ 2.0. See [cortiq.md](../products/cortiq.md).

**"We want to offer BCI therapy for chronic stroke or MS patients."**
recoveriX PRO. See [recoverix.md](../products/recoverix.md).

**"Is this unresponsive patient aware, and can they communicate?"**
mindBEAGLE PRO. See [mindbeagle.md](../products/mindbeagle.md).

**"I need precise triggers and stimulation timing."**
g.STIMbox (16 digital outputs, 14 inputs), g.TRIGbox (visual/auditory/analog/digital trigger detection), g.Estim FES and g.Estim PRO for electrical stimulation.
