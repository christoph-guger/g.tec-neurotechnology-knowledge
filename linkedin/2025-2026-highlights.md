## What worked
- Knowledge and stories beat announcements: the top three posts were about open EEG data, why gamma starts at 35 Hz, and how the Spring School began.
- The BCI & Neurotechnology Spring School is the biggest driver of engagement and new followers every April.
- Series work: five posts from the Mount Kazbek expedition (EEG at −36 °C and 4,200 m) reached about 56,000 impressions.
- Videos with a person, patient or animal wearing the device do better than screens or slides.
