# cortiQ 2.0: functional brain mapping for epilepsy and tumor surgery

**What it is:** an integrated mapping platform that combines real-time high-gamma mapping, electrical cortical stimulation (ECS and hand-held DECS), CCEP network mapping, SEP central sulcus mapping and impedance checks, built on [g.HIamp](g-hiamp.md). Used during awake craniotomy and at the bedside between staged epilepsy surgeries. Source: [cortiQ product page](https://www.gtec.at/product/cortiq-rapid-cortical-mapping/).

## Key specifications
64, 128 or 256 recording channels (grids, strips, depth electrodes; ECoG and sEEG); 24-bit, one ADC per channel, 2,400 Hz per channel; stimulation 0.2–15 mA with g.Estim PRO; FDA clearance K191432.

## Published results
- Mapping validation with no false positives and 1.24% false negatives ([EMBC 2013](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC.2013.6611010)).
- High-gamma mapping matched stimulation mapping with 100% sensitivity and specificity in the frontal lobe ([World Neurosurg 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wneu.2014.08.009)).
- Language mapping: 90.1% sensitivity and 90.0% specificity ([World Neurosurg 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wneu.2016.09.071)).
- High-gamma-guided stimulation needs 40% fewer stimulations ([EMBC 2017](https://doi.org/10.1109/EMBC.2017.8037773)).
- Mapping time reduced from over an hour (stimulation mapping) to a few minutes (g.tec citation list).
