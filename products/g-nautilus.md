# g.Nautilus: wireless EEG

**What it is:** a family of wearable wireless EEG systems for research and clinical use. Sources: [g.Nautilus Research](https://www.gtec.at/product/gnautilus-research-wireless-eeg-system/), [g.Nautilus PRO Flexible](https://www.gtec.at/product/gnautilus-pro-flexible-wireless-clinical-eeg-system/), [g.Nautilus Multi-Purpose](https://www.gtec.at/product/gnautilus-multi-purpose-brain-body-monitoring/), [g.Nautilus with fNIRS](https://www.gtec.at/product/gnautilus-wireless-eeg-fnirs/).

| Model | For | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| g.Nautilus Research | mobile neuroscience, BCI, VR, sports, human factors | 8, 16, 32 or 64 active channels; research use only |
| g.Nautilus PRO Flexible | hospitals, rehabilitation, clinical research | swappable dry and wet electrode strands; CE-certified and FDA-cleared |
| g.Nautilus Multi-Purpose | brain and body monitoring | EEG plus ECG, EMG, EOG, GSR, respiration, SpO₂, pulse, temperature |
| g.Nautilus NIRx / fNIRS | EEG + fNIRS | synchronized EEG and hemodynamics |
| g.Nautilus + Tobii Pro Glasses 3 | real-world eye tracking | EEG, gaze, pupil and scene video |

## Key specifications (Research)
24-bit ADC per channel; 500 Hz (8/16/32 ch) or 250 Hz (64 ch); noise < 0.6 µV RMS (1–30 Hz); under 110 g (8/16/32 ch) or 140 g (64 ch); up to 10 h battery (8/16/32 ch); 2.4 GHz wireless. Electrodes: g.SCARABEO, g.SAHARA (dry/hybrid), g.LADYbird (active wet).

## Published results
- Independent comparison of six wireless EEG systems: g.Nautilus with g.LADYbird had the fewest artifacts and the highest SNR; with g.SAHARA it was the best dry system (Radüntz, Frontiers in Physiology 2018, [doi:10.3389/fphys.2018.00098](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2018.00098)).
- EEG + fNIRS classified consumer preference at 91.8%, versus 78.0% for EEG alone and 66.9% for fNIRS alone (Ramirez, Kaheh, Khalil & George, IEEE CCWC 2022, [doi:10.1109/CCWC54503.2022.9720831](https://doi.org/10.1109/CCWC54503.2022.9720831)).
- MS rehabilitation with recoveriX (g.Nautilus + g.Estim): 6-minute walk +37.3 m ([Front Med 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2025.1661972)).
