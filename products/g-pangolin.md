# g.Pangolin: ultra-high-density EEG, EMG and ECG electrode grids

**What it is:** flexible electrode grids with 16 electrodes each and 8.6 mm spacing (conventional 10-20 EEG: about 60–65 mm), recorded with [g.HIamp](g-hiamp.md) at up to 1,024 synchronized channels. Source: [g.Pangolin product page](https://www.gtec.at/product/pangolin-ultra-high-density-eeg-emg-ecg-electrode-grids/).

## Why it matters
Non-invasive recordings with a spatial resolution that approaches intracranial grids, for brain mapping, source localization, neural decoding and high-density EMG.

## Published results
- Central sulcus localized non-invasively with 95.2% accuracy (256 channels), "comparable to accuracies from invasive intracranial recordings" ([Sci Rep 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-57167-y)).
- Individual finger movements decoded at 64.8% on average, up to 70.6% ([Front Neurosci 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.1009878)).
- Hand gestures: standard 10-10 positions decoded about 10% worse (median) than ultra-high-density EEG ([IEEE NER 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/ner52421.2023.10123901)).
- Visual category decoding at 73% with higher electrode density ([Commun Eng 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44172-026-00611-w)).

## Setup
- 512 channels mounted in about 30 minutes (g.tec product page).
- Grids over the forehead sit on bare skin and need no shaving.
- A full-head montage needs the head shaved.
- For medical uses the head can be shaved only where the grids go, for example over the temporal lobe.

Source: g.tec (Christoph Guger, October 2026).
