# g.USBamp RESEARCH: 16-channel real-time biosignal amplifier

**What it is:** a 16-channel biosignal amplifier for BCI research, closed-loop neuroscience, neurofeedback and multimodal electrophysiology, used in hundreds of BCI studies. Source: [g.USBamp product page](https://www.gtec.at/product/gusbamp-research-16-channel-biosignal-amplifier/).

## Key specifications
| | |
|---|---|
| Channels | 16 monopolar or 8 bipolar per device (software selectable), four independent grounds; several devices can be combined |
| Resolution | 24-bit, one ADC per channel, simultaneous sampling, DC-coupled |
| Sampling rate | up to 38.4 kHz per channel |
| Noise | < 0.4 µV RMS (1–30 Hz) |
| Input impedance | > 1000 GΩ |
| I/O | 8 digital trigger inputs, 4 digital outputs, 2 × 12-bit DAC outputs, USB 2.0 |
| Processing | integrated DSP with bandpass and notch filters, impedance check for active and passive electrodes |

## Works with
g.Pype (Python), g.HIsys (MATLAB/Simulink), g.Recorder, Lab Streaming Layer; combinations with TMS, tDCS and fNIRS.

## Published results with g.USBamp
- P300 vs motor imagery: 89% of 81 people spelled at 80–100% accuracy after about 5 minutes of training, versus 19% with motor imagery ([Neurosci Lett 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neulet.2009.06.045)).
- Dry vs gel electrodes (g.SAHARA vs gel): P300 spelling 90.4% dry vs 91.0% gel; 69.6% vs 72.8% of people reached 100% accuracy ([Front Neurosci 2012](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2012.00060)).
- g.USBamp is the device named most often in the open-access Frontiers papers we read in full (12 of 36).
