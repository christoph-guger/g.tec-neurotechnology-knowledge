# mindBEAGLE PRO: EEG assessment and communication in disorders of consciousness

**What it is:** an EEG-based BCI that tests whether patients with unresponsive wakefulness syndrome (UWS), minimally conscious state (MCS) or locked-in syndrome (LIS) process stimuli and follow commands, and lets selected patients answer YES/NO questions through vibrotactile stimulation or motor imagery. Uses a wireless 16-channel g.Nautilus PRO Flexible EEG; each paradigm takes about 2–4 minutes. Source: [mindBEAGLE product page](https://www.gtec.at/product/mindbeagle-brain-assessment-and-communication/).

**Important:** mindBEAGLE results complement clinical assessment. They should not be the sole basis for diagnosing consciousness disorders, predicting recovery or making treatment decisions, and a negative result does not prove a lack of consciousness.

## Published results
- 9 of 12 locked-in patients communicated with vibrotactile P300, and 2 of 3 completely locked-in patients ([Front Neurosci 2017](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2017.00251)).
- ALS: median accuracy 98% (VT2); 8 of 10 locked-in ALS patients answered at least 7 of 10 questions correctly ([Front Neurosci 2018](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2018.00514)).
- 4 patients repeatedly diagnosed as unresponsive (UWS) by the CRS-R repeatedly showed command following with the BCI ([Front Neurosci 2022](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.959339)).
- Vibrotactile discrimination predicted recovery at 6 months (R = 0.89) ([Clin Neurophysiol 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinph.2018.02.131)).
