# recoveriX PRO: BCI neurorehabilitation after stroke and with multiple sclerosis

**What it is:** a closed-loop rehabilitation system. The patient imagines a hand or foot movement; a wireless 16-channel EEG detects the motor imagery, a virtual avatar shows the movement and functional electrical stimulation (FES) moves the limb. Therapist-guided sessions in hospitals and rehabilitation centers. CE-marked medical device; clinical indications depend on the country. Source: [recoveriX product page](https://www.gtec.at/product/recoverix-neurorehabilitation/).

## Published results
- Chronic stroke, 25 sessions: walking speed +0.19 m/s (95% CI 0.13–0.25, p < 0.001) ([Front Neurosci 2023](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2023.1256077)).
- Upper limb: Fugl-Meyer (FMA-UE) +4.68 points, less wrist and finger spasticity, lasting 6 months ([Front Neurosci 2020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2020.591435)).
- Upper then lower limb training: FMA-UE +4.2, walking speed +0.15 m/s, Barthel Index +5 ([Front Neurosci 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2024.1346607)).
- Multiple sclerosis: Timed Up and Go −15.5%, 25-foot walk −16.4% (p < 0.001), 6-minute walk +37.3 m, effects still present after 6 months ([Front Med 2025](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2025.1661972)).
- Patients averaged at least 82.7% motor imagery accuracy ([Front Robot AI 2018](https://doi.org/10.3389/frobt.2018.00130)).

Effects were reported in patients 10 to 30 years after their stroke (g.tec product page).
